# Life-Insurance Policy Lapse Prediction

**TERV AIML Trainer Project 06 · Trainer: Ashok Kumar P (TFTPL230) · Track B – Classification**
Repository name: `terv-aiml-tftpl230-insurance-policy-lapse-prediction`

> **In one line:** predict which new life-insurance policies will lapse at the 13th month, and choose how many
> customers to call from the cost of a retention call (₹450) versus the cost of a lost policy (₹18,000).

---

## 1. Business problem
A private life insurer issues ~1.2 lakh individual policies a year. About 9% lapse at the 13th month because the
second-year premium is never paid. Each lapse wipes out the first-year commission, issue costs and future margin
(≈ ₹18,000), and 13th-month persistency is a closely watched regulatory and investor metric. The retention desk calls
customers almost at random today, and each outreach costs ≈ ₹450.

**Goal:** by month 10 of a policy, score the risk that the 13th-month premium will not be paid, compare three ways of
handling the 9% class imbalance, and choose the call threshold that **minimises expected cost** (calls + lost policies).

## 2. About the data  ⚠️ please read
The handbook describes the dataset (12,524 rows × 23 columns, `life_policies_13m.csv`) but does not include the file.
This project therefore ships with **a synthetic dataset that follows the handbook exactly**: same 23 columns, same
data-quality problems (1% duplicates, 10% DD-MM-YYYY dates, 18 invalid ages, 25 income placeholders = 999, 12 negative
premiums, missing values, spelling variants in `premium_mode`, `payment_method` and the target) and the lapse patterns
described in the EDA section (affordability, cash payers, bancassurance/ULIP, new agents, engagement, complaints).
It is generated by `src/generate_data.py` (fixed seed, fully reproducible).

**If you have the real CSV**, put it at `data/raw/life_policies_13m.csv` (overwrite the synthetic one) and run the project
again – nothing else needs to change. Numbers in your results will then reflect the real data.

## 5. Approach (follows the handbook)
1. **Data-quality audit** – every issue in the dataset section is counted and reported.
2. **Cleaning** – drop duplicates; parse both date formats; standardise spellings; invalid ages and the `999` income
   placeholder → missing; reversal entries fixed; age/income imputed by *occupation × city tier*; structural fill for
   agent tenure; each decision justified in the notebook.
3. **Feature engineering** – `premium_to_income`, `high_burden_flag`, `autopay_flag`, `new_agent_flag`, `log_premium`,
   `sum_assured_multiple`, `login_flag`, `issue_month`.
4. **EDA** – charts 1–7 with an insight under each.
5. **Time-ordered split** – train on policies issued before 1 Jul 2024; test on Jul – Dec 2024.
6. **Modelling** – Logistic Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM × {no handling, `class_weight`, SMOTE}, evaluated with
   stratified 5-fold CV on PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, recall, precision and F1. SMOTE runs **inside** an `imblearn` Pipeline so it
   never leaks into validation folds.
7. **Selection** – best combination by CV PR-AUC; PR and ROC curves on the test period.
8. **Cost-based threshold** – chosen on **out-of-fold training predictions** by minimising
   `calls × ₹450 + missed lapses × ₹18,000 + called lapses × 70% × ₹18,000` (30% save rate), then confirmed on the test
   period with a confusion matrix and the rupee saving versus making no calls.
9. **Explainability** – cumulative gains chart and permutation importance.

## 6. Success criteria (checked automatically at the end of the run)
| Criterion | Where to see it |
|---|---|
| Test PR-AUC ≥ 3 × the lapse rate | `outputs/reports/summary.json → success_criteria` |
| Recall ≥ 60% at the chosen threshold on Jul – Dec 2024 | same |
| Positive net saving versus making no calls | same |

## 7. Results
Run the project, then read:
* `outputs/reports/insights.md` – 5 insights and 4 recommendations with the actual numbers from your run
* `outputs/reports/summary.json` – dataset stats, best model, threshold, confusion matrix, savings, criteria
* `outputs/reports/metrics.csv` – CV and test metrics for every model/strategy
* `outputs/reports/call_list_test_period.csv` – ranked call list with a short reason for each customer
* `outputs/figures/` – the 13 charts

### Recommendations (themes)
1. **Affordability gate** at underwriting for premiums above ~15% of income.
2. **Auto-pay by default** (NACH / UPI AutoPay) and incentives for cash/cheque and monthly payers to switch.
3. **Fix sales quality at the source** – bancassurance and ULIP suitability, first-year agent mentoring.
4. **Monthly model-driven call list** with reason codes, highest risk first.

## 10. Notes & limitations
* The saved model is fitted on the training period only, so the test-period numbers stay honest. For production, refit on
  all available policies and re-choose the threshold on out-of-fold predictions.
* The ₹450 / ₹18,000 / 30% assumptions live in `src/lapse_utils.py`; change them there to re-run the cost analysis.
* The 0.08 break-even (450 ÷ (0.30 × 18,000)) assumes calibrated probabilities; `class_weight` and SMOTE shift scores
  upward, which is why the threshold is read off the cost curve rather than fixed.
